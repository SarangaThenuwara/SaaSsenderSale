What is Spintax?

In the world of professional outreach, "fingerprinting" is your biggest enemy. If you send 500 identical emails, Gmail's AI will quickly flag the pattern as spam. Spintax (Spin Syntax) allows you to create thousands of variations from a single template.

The Spintax Syntax

Our engine uses the standard curly brace format: {Hello|Hi|Hey}. When the campaign runs, the system randomly selects one of these words for every single recipient.

The Power of Randomized Template Rotation

Beyond simple word changes, SaaS Sender allows you to create up to **3 distinct subject lines and bodies**. In a campaign of 300 emails, our worker will rotate these templates, ensuring that no two consecutive recipients receive exactly the same structure.

Best Practices for 2026

  • Vary your CTA: Instead of "Let's chat," alternate with "Are you free for a call?" or "Let me know if this interests you."
  • Shuffle Paragraphs: Use spintax to rotate entire sentences or paragraph orders to break the AI's pattern recognition.
  • Personalized Attachments: Our system snapshots your CV at the start of the campaign to ensure consistency while rotating the text around it.
