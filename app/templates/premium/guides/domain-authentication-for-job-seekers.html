You have the perfect CV. You have a great cover letter. But your emails are being deleted before an HR manager even sees them. Why? In 2026, most corporate email servers in the UAE use automated authentication checks. If your domain fails these, you are invisible.

What is SPF (Sender Policy Framework)?

SPF is a simple text record in your DNS. It tells the recipient's server: "These are the only IP addresses allowed to send mail on behalf of my domain." For BYOK Gmail users, your SPF record must include Google's servers.

v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all

What is DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)?

Think of DKIM as a wax seal on an ancient envelope. It provides a cryptographic signature that proves the email was not tampered with after it left your "outbox." Without DKIM, your job application looks like "spoofed" spam.

DMARC: The Final Shield

DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance) tells the world what to do if your SPF or DKIM fails. For professional outreach, you should at least have a "p=none" policy to monitor your reputation.
