Security & Encryption

How are my Google API credentials stored?

We use a multi-layered, zero-knowledge encryption architecture. Your JSON credentials are encrypted before they ever reach our persistent database using a Fernet (AES-128 in CBC mode) key that is unique to your user session. We provide the automation logic, but you maintain the cryptographic key.

Can SaaS Sender read my personal emails?

No. When you configure your Google Cloud Project, our guides instruct you to only enable the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send scope. This is a "write-only" permission that allows the engine to send mail, but does not grant permission to read, list, or delete existing messages.

Compliance & Ethics

Is automated cold outreach legal under GDPR?

Yes, provided you are contacting professional email addresses for "Legitimate Interest" and providing a clear way to opt-out. Our platform encourages ethical outreach by allowing you to instantly pause campaigns and manage your lead database with data sovereignty.

What is the BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) model?

BYOK is a paradigm shift in SaaS. Instead of us providing the email accounts (which creates shared risk), you Bring Your Own Key (your Gmail API credentials). This ensures that your sender reputation is 100% yours and is never affected by the actions of other users on the platform.

Technical Operation

What happens if a background worker fails?

Our system uses Celery and Redis for task durability. If a specific email send attempt fails (e.g., due to a temporary Google API outage), the task is automatically retried with exponential backoff, ensuring your campaign eventually reaches every lead in your snapshot.

How does randomized template rotation work?

When you start a campaign, the engine creates a "Frozen Snapshot" of your current settings. For every recipient, the worker randomly selects one of your three variations and applies any spintax. This keeps the output fresh and prevents "pattern-matching" spam filters from identifying your automation.
