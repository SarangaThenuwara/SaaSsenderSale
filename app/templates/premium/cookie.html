This Cookie Policy explains how SaaS Sender ("we", "us", and "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website and use our application. It explains what these technologies are and why we use them, as well as your rights to control our use of them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by online service providers in order to (for example) make their websites or services work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

Why do we use cookies?

We use first-party cookies for several reasons. Some cookies are required for technical reasons in order for our Application to operate, and we refer to these as "essential" or "strictly necessary" cookies.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Session Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary to provide you with services available through our application and to use some of its features, such as access to secure areas. Without these cookies, our application cannot function properly.

  • saas_sender_session: Used to maintain your authenticated session securely.
  • csrf_token: Used to protect against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks.

Third-Party Cookies

We actively minimize the use of third-party cookies. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, trackers, or data miners. Any third-party connections are strictly related to essential infrastructure (such as payment processing via Stripe or analytics if explicitly opted into).

How can I control cookies?

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie rights by setting your preferences in the Cookie Consent Manager. However, please note that if you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website though your access to some functionality and areas of our application will be severely restricted.
