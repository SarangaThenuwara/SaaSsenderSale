Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Our Privacy Commitment

At SaaS Email Sender, your privacy is our top priority. We built this platform with a privacy-first architecture to ensure your data remains secure and under your control at all times.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (for account creation and authentication)
  • Username (derived from your email)
  • Account creation date

Campaign Data

  • Email templates you create
  • CV/resume files you upload
  • Recruiter contact information you import
  • Campaign statistics and sending history

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide and maintain the email automation service
  • Send emails on your behalf through your Gmail account
  • Display campaign statistics and performance metrics
  • Improve and optimize our platform

OAuth2 & Gmail Integration

We never see or store your Gmail password.

Our platform uses Google's OAuth2 protocol for authentication. This means:

  • Your Gmail credentials remain exclusively within Google's infrastructure
  • We only receive temporary access tokens that you can revoke at any time
  • All communication with Gmail uses encrypted connections
  • You maintain full control over what permissions you grant

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely:

  • All data stored in encrypted databases
  • Industry-standard encryption for data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication requirements

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

The only external service we interact with is Google Gmail (via OAuth2) to send emails on your behalf. We do not share your data with advertisers, marketers, or data brokers.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we store about you
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export: Download your campaign data at any time
  • Revoke: Disconnect Gmail access through your Google Account settings

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through your account dashboard or email us directly.
