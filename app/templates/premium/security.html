Discover how SaaS Sender utilizes Zero-Knowledge encryption and infrastructure segregation to protect your mission-critical outreach data.

Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

Our revolutionary architecture shifts control back to you. We act strictly as an orchestration engine, while your native Google Workspace API tokens serve as the ignition key. We never mandate shared IP relays, fully isolating your sender reputation from our broader user base.

OAuth2 Authorization Only

SaaS Sender never views, stores, or requests your underlying Gmail password. The industry-standard OAuth2 flow grants granular, scoped access specifically for automated drafting and sending—nothing else. You can unilaterally revoke this access at any time from your Google Dashboard.

Rate Limiting & Abuse Prevention

A core vector for SPAM classification is erratic sending velocity. Our decentralized worker clusters employ deeply integrated Rate Limiters and intelligent queuing to emulate organic human drafting patterns, effectively shielding domains against algorithmic bans or soft bounces.

Incident Response & Recovery

Our infrastructure natively supports point-in-time recovery with continuous snapshots hosted in immutable storage buckets. In the unlikely event of an anomaly or data intrusion attempt, our automated tripwires trigger instantaneous environment lockdowns and alert our internal SecOps team sub-minute.
